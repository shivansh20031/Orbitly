About

Orbitly

Solve it once. Watch it come back around.

Most people solve a DSA problem, feel great, and forget the approach within weeks. Orbitly brings each one back just before it fades.

How it works

  1. Solve a problem anywhereLeetCode, a book, a contest. It all counts.
  2. Log it in OrbitlySave your approach, time and space complexity, and the mistakes you made.
  3. Review it when its planet glows orangeTry to recall the approach, reveal your notes, then rate how well you remembered.

Your data

Everything is stored only in this browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. To back up or move to another device, open Settings (the ⚙ button at the top), choose Export JSON, then use Import JSON on the other device. Clearing your browser data erases your library, so export now and then.

Tips

  • Review daily. A few minutes each day beats a long session once a week.
  • Write notes in your own words. If you can explain it simply, you understand it.
  • Add the mistake you made. Your past errors are the best hints for next time.
